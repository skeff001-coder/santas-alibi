Common questions

The sound didn't play. Turn the ringer on (the silent switch mutes lock-screen sound), turn the volume up, and allow notifications for Santa's Alibi in Settings.

How do I restore my Pro Pass? Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore purchase.

Does the app collect my photos? No. Photos stay on your device.
